Following months of intensive diplomatic efforts, we have solidified the "Transnational Archonate".

### Official Registration: European Citizens' Initiative 2026/000012

Today, October 6, 2026, the European Union's institutions have been legally bound to review and admit our initiative. We formally announce that the European Commission in Strasbourg has registered our Citizens' Initiative:

**"Creation of the European Digital District (.IA) for the Governance of Sovereign Artificial Intelligence"**

* **EU Docket:** ECI nº 2026/000012
* **Community Legal Act:** Commission Implementing Decision C(2026) 7098 final.

The European Commission has acknowledged its legal competence to legislate on internet infrastructure and the `.ia` domain under Articles 16, 114, 172, and 173 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union (TFEU). This transforms `.ia` into a matter of **state and community sovereignty**.
